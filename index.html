01/ about

Who I am

I got into security because I believe everyone deserves to feel safe online. I'm a graduate student pursuing an MSc in Information System Technology with a concentration in Information Assurance, specializing in digital forensics and penetration testing.

My work spans incident investigation, offensive security assessments, and helping organizations close the gaps attackers look for. I work with industry-standard tools — Cellebrite UFED, Magnet Axiom, FTK Imager, Volatility, Autopsy, and Burp Suite — across computers, mobile devices, drones, volatile memory, and network traffic.

I hold the Certified AppSec Practitioner (CAP) and Certified Network Security Practitioner (CNSP) certifications, with hands-on ethical hacking training from Cisco — and more on the way, because this field never stops evolving and neither do I.
